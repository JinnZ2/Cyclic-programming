## 🎨 Syntax Reference

### Operators

|Symbol|Meaning                   |Example           |
|------|--------------------------|------------------|
|`∇`   |Field gradient/interaction|`∇F(a↔b)`         |
|`∇²`  |Laplacian (spatial)       |`∇²F(...)`        |
|`∇³`  |Multi-field network       |`∇³F(a↔b↔c)`      |
|`↔`   |Bidirectional exchange    |`a↔b`             |
|`⇄`   |Symbiotic relationship    |`a⇄b`             |
|`→`   |Unidirectional flow       |`a→b`             |
|`∮`   |Closed cycle/regeneration |`∮regenerate(...)`|
|`∮^n` |Fractal recursion         |`∮^3(...)`        |
|`∂`   |Partial derivative/change |`∂E/∂t`           |
|`⊗`   |Quantum tensor product    |`⊗(a,b)`          |
|`~`   |Resonance/approximation   |`~(a ≈ b)`        |
|`≈`   |Approximately equal       |`freq1 ≈ freq2`   |
|`|`   |Constraint separator      |`...` `|∂E/∂t=0`  |

### Constraints

|Constraint|Meaning                   |
|----------|--------------------------|
|`∂E/∂t=0` |Energy conserved over time|
|`∂S/∂t≥0` |Entropy must increase     |
|`ΔE=0`    |Total energy unchanged    |
